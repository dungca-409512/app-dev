## HOMELAND
[Link to IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1796960/)

# My favorite series, **Homeland** follows *Carrie Mathison*, a CIA officer who suspects that a returning U.S Marine, held captive for years, may have been turned into a terrorist threat to the nation. 

**Fun Facts about the show**
- It's based on an Israeli series called Prisoners of War (Hatufim), created by Gideon Raff.
- The US version was developed by Howard Gordon and Alex Gansa, who both worked on 24

**Awards**
- Season 1 of the film won the big Emmys in 2012: Outstanding Drama Series, Best Actress for [Claire Danes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Danes) (the show's protagonist), and Best Supporting Actor for [Damian Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damian_Lewis). It also won Golden Globes for the series, Claire, and Damian

**Cast trivia**
- Claire Danes got famous as a teen in My So-Called Life and played Juliet opposite Leonardo DiCaprio in Romeo + Juliet.
- Damian Lewis is actually British, so that’s an American accent you’re hearing. He also played Major Richard Winters in Band of Brothers.
- Morena Baccarin (Jessica) was in Firefly and later played Vanessa in Deadpool.
- Claire Danes’ “cry face” became a meme, and she’s said she was a bit self-conscious about it.

**Behind the Scenes**
- The early seasons were filmed in Charlotte, North Carolina, which stood in for Washington, DC. Later seasons moved to places like South Africa, Germany, and Morocco.
- The jazzy theme music is tied to Carrie’s love of jazz, which is a big part of her character.
- Barack Obama was reportedly a fan of the show.
